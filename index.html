Profile

Institutional Facilitation Built on Integrity, Speed & Precision

We bridge primary refinery allocations, major state titleholders and qualified international buyers through disciplined commercial structure, independent verification and globally aligned documentation.

Q8 Petrol is an established oil & gas and global commodity trading facilitation entity, operating from a strategic dual presence across Kuwait — our Middle East trading hub — and Sri Lanka, our Indian Ocean maritime and compliance headquarters.

This positioning allows us to deliver comprehensive trade execution across global ports, coordinating international commodity supply contracts, direct refinery allocations, vessel chartering and independent third-party inspection under a single, accountable structure.

Every trade we facilitate is built on verified sourcing and internationally recognised inspection standards, backed by a network of refineries, mandates and logistics partners developed through sustained engagement across the GCC, Asia, Europe and Indian Ocean trade corridors.

“ Where others broker on assumption, Q8 Petrol facilitates on evidence.

Integrity in counterparty vetting, speed in trade execution and precision in documentation, inspection and delivery remain central to how we operate.

PROFILE

Institutional facilitation
built on precision.

Integrity. Speed. Precision. Three principles behind every transaction we facilitate.

Q8 Petrol is an established oil & gas and global commodity trading facilitation entity, operating from a strategic dual presence across Kuwait, the Middle East trading hub, and Sri Lanka, our Indian Ocean maritime and compliance headquarters.

This positioning allows us to deliver comprehensive trade execution across global ports, coordinating international commodity supply contracts, direct refinery allocations, vessel chartering, and independent third-party inspection under a single, accountable structure.

We bridge the gap between primary refinery allocations, major state titleholders, and qualified international buyers — structuring every transaction on institutional banking compliance, independent testing, and transparent maritime shipping.

Profile

Institutional Facilitation Built on Integrity, Speed & Precision

We bridge primary refinery allocations, titleholders and qualified international buyers through disciplined commercial structure, independent and globally aligned documentation.

Q8 Petrol (ME-SL) is an established oil & gas and global commodity trading facilitation entity, operating from a strategic dual presence across Kuwait — our Middle East trading hub — and Sri Lanka, our Indian Ocean maritime and compliance headquarters.

This positioning allows us to deliver comprehensive trade execution across global ports, coordinating international commodity supply contracts, refinery allocations and vessel chartering under a single, accountable structure.

Every trade we facilitate is built on verified sourcing and internationally recognised inspection standards, backed by a network of refineries, mandates and logistics partners developed through sustained engagement across Americas, Europe, Middle East, Asia, Oceania and Africa.

“ Where others broker on assumption, Q8 Petrol (ME-SL) facilitates on evidence.

Integrity in counterparty vetting, speed in trade execution and precision in documentation, inspection and delivery remain central to how we operate.
